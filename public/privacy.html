隱私權政策

工作排程確認系統 · 最後更新：2026-10-14

我們存了什麼

  • 你的排程：項目、會議、循環規則、專案與甘特圖、每日記錄與專案筆記、標籤、假日設定。這些是你自己輸入的。
  • 帳號資料：email、密碼的雜湊（PBKDF2，永遠不存明文）、註冊時間、帳號狀態。
  • 第三方登入：你選擇 Google 或 Sign in with Apple 時，會儲存該服務提供的 subject、email 與登入來源，用來把不同裝置連到同一個本服務帳號。Apple refresh token 只用於你刪除帳號時撤銷 Apple credential。
  • App 交易資料：Apple 提供的 app 交易識別碼與購買日期，用來限制同一個 Apple 帳號重複建立 app 帳號。不含你的 Apple ID、姓名或付款資訊。
  • Pro 訂閱資料：訂閱商品識別碼、Apple 的原始交易識別碼、到期日與退款／撤銷日期，用來判斷 Pro 權利。付款資料由 Apple 處理，我們不會取得信用卡資料。
  • 登入中的裝置：裝置類型（例如「iPhone app」或「Chrome on Windows」）與最後使用時間。不記錄 IP 位址。
  • 推播資料：你允許推播後，會儲存 Apple APNs 裝置 token、環境與最後使用時間；你登出、取消註冊或 token 失效時會刪除。
  • 分享記錄：你分享給誰、誰動過你分享的項目，保留 90 天。

存在哪裡

Cloudflare（Workers、D1 資料庫、R2 儲存）。資料庫位於亞太區。每天自動備份一次到 R2，保留最近 14 份；備份不含密碼雜湊。

誰看得到

  • 你的排程只有你自己看得到，以及你主動分享給的帳號（只看到你分享的那幾個項目）。
  • 管理者看得到帳號的 email、狀態與操作記錄，看不到任何人的排程內容。
  • 我們不販售、不交換、不用於廣告。沒有第三方追蹤或分析工具。

會送到第三方的資料

  • AI 小幫手（你按下去才會用）：你當下的排程摘要會送到 DeepSeek 的 API 產生回答。不用這個功能就不會送。
  • 提醒信與驗證碼：透過 AgentMail 寄到你註冊的信箱。
  • Apple：App 內的購買與訂閱由 Apple StoreKit 處理；Apple 也會把已簽章的訂閱狀態通知送到本服務，用來更新續訂、到期與退款。
  • Google：你選擇 Google 登入時，Google 會提供已驗證的登入 token；本服務只保留必要的帳號 subject 與 email，不會取得 Google 密碼。
  • 推播：開啟推播時，通知內容與 APNs token 會送到 Apple Push Notification service。
  • 字型：頁面會向 Google Fonts 取得字型檔；離線或封鎖時退回系統字型，功能不受影響。

保留多久

帳號與排程會保留到你刪除帳號。登入 session 會在期限到期或登出後失效；分享與管理記錄保留 90 天，寄信記錄保留 30 天，備份最多保留 14 份。訂閱事件只保留必要的交易識別碼與權利狀態。第三方登入身分在帳號刪除時刪除，Apple credential 同時要求撤銷。推播 token 在取消註冊或被 Apple 判定失效時刪除。

怎麼刪除

在 app 或網頁版的「我的帳號」分頁最下面按「刪除我的帳號」，輸入密碼確認。帳號、雲端上的所有排程、分享、行事曆訂閱與提醒設定會立即刪除；每日備份裡的副本會在 14 天輪替後自動消失。

你也可以隨時在「我的帳號」匯出一份完整的 JSON 備份。

Privacy Policy

Work Schedule · Last updated 2026-10-14

What we store

  • Your schedule: items, meetings, recurrence rules, projects and Gantt charts, daily notes and project notes, tags, holiday settings. All of it is what you typed in.
  • Account data: email, a password hash (PBKDF2; plaintext is never stored), registration time, account status.
  • Third-party sign-in: if you choose Google or Sign in with Apple, we store the provider subject, email and provider name to connect your devices to the same Work Schedule account. An Apple refresh token is used only to revoke the Apple credential when you delete the account.
  • App transaction data: an app transaction identifier and purchase date from Apple, used to prevent repeated account creation with the same Apple account. We do not receive your Apple ID, name or payment details.
  • Pro subscription data: the product identifier, Apple original transaction identifier, expiry date, and refund/revocation date, used to determine Pro entitlement. Apple processes payment; we do not receive card details.
  • Signed-in devices: device type (for example "iPhone app" or "Chrome on Windows") and last-used time. IP addresses are not recorded.
  • Push data: if you allow push notifications, we store Apple's APNs device token, environment and last-used time; it is deleted when you sign out, unregister, or the token becomes invalid.
  • Sharing log: who you shared with and who changed a shared item, kept for 90 days.

Where it lives

Cloudflare (Workers, D1 database, R2 storage). The database is in the Asia-Pacific region. A backup is taken daily to R2 and the latest 14 are kept; backups do not contain password hashes.

Who can see it

  • Only you can see your schedule, plus the accounts you choose to share with (they see only the items you shared).
  • Administrators can see account emails, status and the admin activity log. They cannot see anyone's schedule content.
  • We do not sell, trade or use your data for advertising. There are no third-party trackers or analytics.

Data sent to third parties

  • AI assistant (only when you use it): a summary of your current schedule is sent to DeepSeek's API to produce the answer. Nothing is sent if you don't use it.
  • Reminder and verification emails: delivered through AgentMail to the address you registered with.
  • Apple: in-app purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple StoreKit. Apple also sends signed subscription-status notifications to this service so renewals, expiry and refunds can update your entitlement.
  • Google: when you choose Google sign-in, Google provides a verified sign-in token; we store only the necessary subject and email and never receive your Google password.
  • Push notifications: when enabled, notification content and the APNs token are sent to Apple's Push Notification service.
  • Fonts: the page fetches font files from Google Fonts; offline or blocked, it falls back to system fonts with no loss of function.

Retention

Your account and schedule remain until you delete your account. Sign-in sessions expire or are revoked when you sign out; sharing and admin logs are kept for 90 days, mail logs for 30 days, and at most 14 backups are retained. Subscription events retain only the transaction identifiers and entitlement state needed to operate subscriptions. Third-party identities are deleted with the account, and the Apple credential is also sent for revocation. Push tokens are deleted when you unregister or Apple reports them invalid.

How to delete

At the bottom of the "My account" tab in the app or on the web, press "Delete my account" and confirm with your password. The account, every schedule in the cloud, shares, calendar feed and reminder settings are deleted immediately; copies inside the daily backups expire after the 14-day rotation.

You can also export a full JSON backup from "My account" at any time.
